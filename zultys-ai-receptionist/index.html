Minimize Risk While Maintaining Accuracy

Zultys AI Receptionist is practical, purpose-built AI designed to reduce risk — not create it — enabling AI adoption without customer anxiety about compliance or incorrect information.

Configure Data Sources and Role-Based Access

Administrators define what information the AI can access, which roles can use it, and how it responds, allowing organizations to enforce security policies and compliance requirements while maintaining operational flexibility.

No Hallucinations!
